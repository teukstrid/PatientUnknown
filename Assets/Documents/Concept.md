# Concept - Final Project

**Name:** Astrid Skillingstad
**Game title:** The Asylum
**Type:** 3D

---

## Player Fantasy
The feeling the player is really there for, as one sentence: "I am ___."
In a 5-minute game, it's one moment, not a whole adventure.

> I am a patient, wrongfully admitted to an asylum, who must sneak past the menacing nurses to find my chance to escape.

## Pillars
Three short rules that protect the fantasy. A good pillar says no to something.

1. Observe before acting
2. Every encounter teaches something
3. The environment tells a story

## Core Loop
What the player does again and again, as 3 or 4 verbs.

> Observe nurses -> find an opening -> sneak past or incapacitate -> reach the elevator

## Win and/or Lose
How does it end? You need at least one.

- Win: Reach the elevator to escape the asylum
- Lose: Get caught by the nurse, and wake up back in your patient room

## Risk
Something that could stop your game working, or make it late. Then: how will you test it first?

> Making an AI behavior that feels both smart, yet conquerable. 

## Opportunity
Something that could make your game special.

> Every obstacle has more than one solution. Players can observe patrols, choose different routes, or discover alternative ways past the nurses.
