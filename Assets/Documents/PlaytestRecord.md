## How to Run It
1. Hand over the game. Say only: "Please play it."
2. Say nothing while they play. Don't help, don't explain. (The silent walk, Class 8.)
3. Write what they **did**, not what they **said**.
4. When they finish, you may ask one question.

## ~~One~~three Change(s) I Would Make

What did you see, and what would you change because of it?

> I saw that many playtesters had a hard time knowing when the nurse was chasing them, so I would change the juice accompanying the chase state.
> I saw that many playtesters missed or had a hard time seeing the objective text, so I would change the placent of the text or coloring.
> I saw that the playtesters wanted more knowledge of what they could and couldn't do, so I would add a tutorial text at the beginning and potentially toolbar on syringe to help more.
